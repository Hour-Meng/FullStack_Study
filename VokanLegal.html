VOKAN Launch Checklist

Comprehensive Startup Compliance & Platform Requirements (2026)

1. Legal & Business Infrastructure

Corporate Shield & Terms
  • Business Structure: Establish an LLC or C-Corp to insulate personal assets from liabilities.
  • Terms of Service (ToS): Rules specifying user guidelines, disclaimers, and clear ownership over app IP.
  • Brand Protection: Trademark "VOKAN" and corporate logos early to secure your identity.
Privacy & Data Governance
  • Privacy Policy: Explicit breakdown of what data is collected, why it's collected, and data-sharing transparency.
  • Global Regulations: Hard compliance with GDPR (EU data rights) and CCPA (California consumer privacy).

2. Platform Ecosystem Mandates

iOS Apple App Store
  • Technical Stack: Mandated builds using Xcode 26 and the iOS 26 SDK.
  • Privacy Labels: Manual configuration of Data Nutrition Labels in App Store Connect.
  • App Tracking Transparency: Active user opt-in required before tracking data across external apps.
  • Subscription Paywalls: Explicit clarity on total pricing and simplified cancellation paths.
  • Account: $99 annual fee; subject to comprehensive human UI/UX reviews.
Android Google Play Store
  • Build Standards: Mandatory Android App Bundle (AAB) targets hitting Android 16 (API level 36).
  • Closed Testing Protocol: Mandatory testing window of 20 continuous testers for a minimum of 14 days.
  • Permissions Model: Strict review of necessary permissions; no broad device folder access allowed.
  • Account: One-time $25 registration fee backed by rigorous developer identity verification.

3. Health & Fitness Data Compliance

Data Silos & Storage
  • Explicit Consent UI: Prominent in-app verification interface capturing active user consent before compiling workout stats.
  • Database Isolation: Strict database Row Level Security (RLS) guaranteeing users only read/write their personal entries.
  • Data Retention: Built-in mechanism to purge user logs completely upon account deletion or erasure requests.
Platform-Specific Health Rules
  • Google Health Connect: Strict feature-specific justifications required in the developer console for every tracking parameter.
  • Medical Disclaimer: Public store listing metadata must specify that VOKAN is explicitly not a diagnostic medical device.
  • Apple HealthKit Policy: Total ban on deploying gathered training or biometric data to ad networks or data brokers.

4. Technical & Launch Readiness

Core Operations
  • Encryption Standards: Complete end-to-end encryption in transit via TLS 1.2+ alongside robust password hashing.
  • Telemetry: Real-time production runtime monitoring using Crashlytics or Sentry for fast patching.
  • Asset Kit: Exactly scaled store display visuals, high-fidelity mockups, and dynamic app preview recordings.
